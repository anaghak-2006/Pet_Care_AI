💡 General Food Safety

  • Keep fresh water available throughout the day.
  • Use food appropriate for your pet's species and life stage.
  • Avoid chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions and garlic for dogs.
  • Never give cats dog food as their regular diet.
  • Ask your vet before changing diets or feeding homemade meals.
